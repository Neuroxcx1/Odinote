ODDINOTE - PLEASE READ BEFORE INSTALLING

By installing and using Oddinote you agree to the following.

1. Usage statistics

Oddinote sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics: when the app
is opened and for how long, which kinds of node are created, whether live
rooms, view-only links and Google Drive work, and the approximate country and
city that Google works out from your internet connection. Google also gives
this device a random identifier.

Your name, your email, the names of your projects and anything you write or
put in your notes are never sent.

Details: https://github.com/Neuroxcx1/Odinote/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

2. Automatic updates

Oddinote checks GitHub for new versions. If "Update automatically" is on in
Settings (it is by default), new versions download by themselves and install
when you close the app. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

3. Your projects

Your projects stay on your computer. They only leave it if you connect Google
Drive, open a live room or create a view-only link.
