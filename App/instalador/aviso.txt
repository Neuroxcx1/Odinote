ODDINOTE - PLEASE READ BEFORE INSTALLING

By installing and using Oddinote you agree to the following.

1. Usage statistics

Oddinote sends pseudonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics: when the
app is opened and for how long, which kinds of node are created, whether live
sessions and Google Drive work, and the approximate country and city that
Google works out from your internet connection. Outside the EU/EEA, the UK
and Switzerland, Google also gives this device a random identifier; in those
countries nothing is stored on the device.

Your name, your email, the names of your projects and anything you write or
put in your notes are never sent.

Full privacy policy: https://oddinote-web.vercel.app/privacy.html

2. Automatic updates

Oddinote checks GitHub for new versions. If "Update automatically" is on in
Settings (it is by default), new versions download by themselves and install
when you close the app. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

3. Your projects

Your projects stay on your computer. They only leave it if you connect Google
Drive, open a live session or create a view-only link. If you sign in with
Google, the app receives your name, email and profile picture.

4. Age

Oddinote is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 16, or under
the age of digital consent where you live, install and use it only with the
permission of a parent or guardian. The app never asks for your age, name or
email, so it cannot tell who is using it.
