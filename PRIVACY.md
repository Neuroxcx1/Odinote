# Privacy — Oddinote

## Usage statistics

The Windows/Mac app and the official web app (`neuroxcx1.github.io/Odinote`) send **anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics**. They help to know whether Oddinote is used and which parts are useful.

What is sent:

- That the app was opened, on which platform (desktop or web), and for how many minutes it is used.
- Which **kinds** of node are created (note, image, board…), how deep you go into boards, and how many strokes a saved drawing has.
- Whether live rooms, view-only links and putting a project online in Google Drive work or fail (with a short error message when they fail).

What Google adds by itself:

- Your **approximate country and city**, worked out from your internet connection.
- A **random identifier for each device**, so that a returning device is not counted as a new one.

What is **never** sent: your name, your email, the names of your projects, or anything you write or put in your notes.

Copies of Oddinote that are run from the source code (or a fork) send nothing.

## Your projects

Your projects stay on your computer (in your vault folder or in the app's data). They only leave it if **you** connect Google Drive, open a live room, or create a view-only link, and then only to do that.
